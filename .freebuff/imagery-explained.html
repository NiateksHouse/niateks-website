The gap analysis

What separates today’s page from high quality

Six upgrades, in order of visual impact. Two are yours (assets), one is blocked on data, three happen automatically when the files arrive.

G1

Real photography replaces gradient slots

Action now
Today10 slots show warm gradient placeholders labeled “Image pending”. Composition and ratios are final — the frames are simply empty.
→
TargetEvery slot filled per its card above: 2 AI-generated frames (exact negative-space control) + 8 licensed stock images, all warm-neutral, all faceless, all on-brand.

Action now

Quality guardrails

Rules that protect the quality once images arrive

Applies to every search, every generation, every crop — no exceptions.

watermarks & logostext inside imagesCGI / plastic sheenHDR oversaturationcool blue castgold & marble luxury clichésfactory floors & machinerygiant fabric rollsspa orchids & candlespeople looking at camerahandshakescluttered propping
  • 1No identifiable faces anywhere — hands-only, from-behind or faceless crops. Brand-safe, more editorial, and no model-release exposure.
  • 2One photographic language — soft directional daylight, warm ivory/sand/olive palette, tactile material focus. Any image that fights it gets rejected, however good.
  • 3Record the source URL of every stock image even where attribution isn’t required — one row per file in the sourcing list.
  • 4Never fake products — slots 10–12 wait for real NIATEKS photography, however long it takes.
  • 5Contrast over images is checked, not assumed — the craft-band scrim exists so white text passes AA; the hero’s left wall must stay calm for the same reason.

Definition of done

“High quality” means all of this

  • All 10 asset slots filled with on-brand photography
  • Hero typography zone calm & readable on both 16:9 and 4:5 crops
  • No key object cropped at 1440 / 768 / 375
  • AVIF/WebP served, JPG fallback in place
  • srcset + sizes on every responsive image
  • LCP image uses fetchpriority="high"
  • Explicit width/height — CLS ≈ 0
  • Lazy loading on everything below the fold
  • White text over dark-band images passes AA under scrim
  • Final alt text on every image; decorative layers aria-hidden
  • Source URL + license recorded for every stock file
  • No faces, no logos, no banned patterns — avoid-list sweep clean
  • Zero console errors after the swap
  • Product slots still honest (real data or labeled placeholder)
